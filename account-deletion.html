Swadharma – Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Overview

You can close your Swadharma account and request deletion of your personal data at any time, from inside the app or by contacting us. This page explains how, what is deleted, what we may keep, and what happens to your wallet balance.

2. How to delete your account

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete Account. Read the information shown, choose a reason, tick the confirmation box and tap Delete.
  • By request: if you cannot access the app, email us from your registered contact details at <support email> with the subject “Delete my account” and your registered phone number.

Your account closes as soon as you confirm. Closing an account is permanent and cannot be undone.

3. When you cannot delete yet

The app may ask you to wait if a consultation or a booking is still in progress, or if a payment or refund is pending. Once it is completed, you can delete your account.

4. What is deleted

  • Profile details: name, phone number, email, date, time and place of birth.
  • Saved kundli and family profiles.
  • Chat, call and consultation content, session summaries and notifications.
  • Device and push-notification identifiers.

You are signed out on all devices, and the phone number can no longer be used to log in to this account.

5. What we may keep

We keep only what the law or a legitimate business need requires, for the period required:

  • Payment, invoice and wallet transaction records, for tax, accounting and audit purposes.
  • Records needed to resolve disputes, complaints or fraud, and to comply with legal obligations.

This data is kept securely, is not used for marketing, and is deleted when the retention period ends. Retention period: <e.g. up to 180 days for financial records>.

6. Wallet balance and credits

  • Paid (cash) balance: the app shows your remaining paid balance before you confirm. Refunds, where applicable, are processed to the original payment method as described in our refund policy.
  • Promotional credits and offers have no cash value and lapse when the account is closed.

7. Astrologers and other users

Deleting your account does not delete the astrologer's own records of a past consultation that are needed for their payout and compliance. Your personal details are removed from them wherever possible.

8. Account blocked or suspended

If your account has been blocked or suspended by us, you cannot log in. You can still request deletion by contacting support.
