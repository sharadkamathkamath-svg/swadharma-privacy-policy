Privacy Policy

Swadharma Partner App (for astrologers) · Effective date: 3 November 2026

1. Introduction

Swadharma Partner ("the App") is operated by 3ioNetra Faith & Technology Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Swadharma", "we", "us"). The App is for registered astrologers ("partners") who offer consultations to devotees on the Swadharma platform. This policy explains what personal data we collect from partners, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

By registering for or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and profile: name, mobile number, email address, profile photo, bio, languages, skills, experience and areas of expertise.
  • Verification (KYC) and payments: identity and tax documents (such as PAN), and bank account details used to pay you.
  • Content you create: chat messages, private notes on devotees, remedies, review replies, support tickets, dispute messages and appeals.
  • Photos and videos: images you upload from your gallery or capture in the App, such as your profile photo and KYC documents.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data: device model, operating system version, app version, language setting and a push-notification token.
  • Usage data: online and offline status, sessions handled, earnings, ratings and account-standing records.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and error logs, used to find and fix problems.
  • Call and chat data: technical connection data for audio calls and messages. Sessions may be recorded for safety and dispute resolution, as described in section 4.

What we do not collect: the App does not access your device's location (GPS). It also does not read your contacts or SMS.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your partner account, verify your identity and approve you to take sessions.
  • To connect you with devotees and provide chat and call consultation services.
  • To calculate earnings, process withdrawals and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • To show your profile, ratings and reviews to devotees.
  • To keep the platform safe: handling reports, disputes, discipline actions and appeals, and preventing fraud and abuse. Sessions may be recorded and reviewed for this purpose.
  • To send service notifications and respond to support requests.
  • To monitor performance, fix errors and improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After you delete your account we remove or anonymise personal data, except records we must keep for a limited period: payout, wallet and tax records (8 years), KYC records (5 years), and dispute, safety and discipline records (3 to 5 years). The full rules are in our Account Deletion Policy.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect your data. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private and tell us promptly if you suspect misuse.

8. Your rights and choices

Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete your data and account (Profile > Delete account, or through Help & Support);
  • withdraw consent, which may limit the features available to you;
  • turn off notifications or revoke permissions in your phone's settings;
  • raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (section 11).

9. Children

The App is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. We will notify you in the App of any material change.
