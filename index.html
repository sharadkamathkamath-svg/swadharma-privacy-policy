5. Recordings, retention, storage and security

5.1 Session recordings. Astro chats are stored, and voice and video calls are recorded, so that we can resolve complaints, check billing, and detect and act on misconduct by users or astrologers. Automated tools scan sessions for abuse, sexual content and sharing of contact details; flagged sessions and a random sample are reviewed by trained trust and safety staff under access controls. You are told this before your first session and the session screen shows a recording indicator.

5.2 How long we keep data.

Data Kept for
Account and profile While your account is active; erased after you delete it, or after 3 years with no activity (with 48 hours' notice first)
Astro chats and call/video recordings [180] days after the session, then deleted
Recordings flagged for misconduct or under complaint Until the matter and any appeal or legal proceeding is closed
Full puja video 30 days in your profile; our archive copy [90] days
Orders, payments, invoices, Wallet ledger 8 years, as required by tax and company law
Support tickets and grievances [3] years after closure
Security and access logs At least 1 year, as the DPDP Rules require

After these periods data is deleted or irreversibly de-identified, unless a law or court order requires us to keep it longer.

5.3 Where it is stored. Our main servers are in India (AWS, Mumbai). Some vendors in section 4 may process data abroad.

5.4 Security. We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-based access with logging, OTP-based login, masked phone numbers in calls, and regular security reviews. Payment data is handled by our PCI-DSS certified payment partner.

5.5 If there is a breach. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay what happened, the likely impact and what you can do, and we will report it to the Data Protection Board of India, with a detailed report within 72 hours, as the DPDP Rules require.

6. Your rights, consent, children and family members

6.1 Your rights. Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;

  • correct, complete or update it (most fields directly in Profile);

  • ask us to erase it, except what the law requires us to keep (section 5.2);

  • withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, from Profile > Privacy or by writing to us. Withdrawal stops future processing on that basis but does not undo past processing; some services may no longer work (for example, you cannot take consultations if you withdraw consent to session recording);

  • nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable;

  • complain to our Grievance Officer, and if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India.

We respond to rights requests within [30] days and never later than 90 days. We may ask you to verify your identity first.

6.2 Marketing choices. Unsubscribe from promotional WhatsApp, SMS or email at any time with the opt-out in the message or in Profile > Notifications. Service messages (OTP, orders, proof videos) continue while you use the services.

6.3 Children. The Platform is for adults. We do not knowingly let anyone under 18 open an account. A parent or guardian may add a child's name and birth details to their family profile for a reading or puja; by doing so the parent confirms they are the child's parent or lawful guardian and consents for the child. We do not track children's behaviour or target ads at them. If you believe a child has an account, write to the Grievance Officer and we will delete it. [Counsel: check verifiable parental consent under DPDP Rule 10.]

6.4 Family members' data. We save a family member's details only after you confirm in the app that the adult family member has agreed, or that you are the parent or lawful guardian consenting for them. We keep a record of that confirmation and use the details only for the readings and pujas you book for them. Family members may write to us to access, correct or delete their details, or to withdraw consent.

6.5 Data you share with an astrologer. Astrologers are bound by contract to use what you tell them only for your session and not to keep, share or contact you outside the Platform. Please share only what is needed for your reading; avoid sharing other people's sensitive matters.
